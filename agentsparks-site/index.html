What it does

One quiet suggestion, with the reason shown.

Notice the gap

Compares your recent activity with people who share your tastes, and with what you used to do and dropped. Needs you mute, and sensitive topics, are never touched.

Build a journey

A short path across a video, a show, a course and, last, a product. Free steps come first. Nothing is sponsored.

Stay out of the way

One card, no feed, no countdowns. "Not for me" and "Mute" are always one tap, and every card says why you are seeing it.

Promises, enforced in code

The rules are not left to the agents' judgment.

  • Money, security and compliance actions are blocked and handed to a person.
  • Your data is used only for sources you consented to, one by one.
  • Every decision is written to an audit log; one command halts everything.
  • Messages to outsiders are drafts until a person approves them.
  • Health, money troubles, religion, politics and other sensitive needs are never targeted.
  • No paid placement and no dark patterns, ever.

Where things stand

Early, and open about it.

The recommender and the agent team work end to end on simulated users. There are no real users yet, so there are no real results to show. The agents are rule-based and self-tuning today; specialist trained models come once there is real data to train on.

  1. Real data. Upload consented exports from Netflix, Google Takeout and order history, with a delete-my-data command.
  2. Learned models. A free open-weight embedding model and a recommender trained on real events, always checked against the popularity baseline.
  3. Specialist agents. Small open models fine-tuned for each role, with the safety rules kept outside the models.
  4. Real experiments. Randomized holdouts on real traffic, tracking mutes and regret as well as clicks.
