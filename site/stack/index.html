AgentSparks · agent infrastructure

Reliable multi-agent systems at a fraction of the cost.

AgentSparks is the infrastructure under your agents: cheap models first, every result checked independently, side effects exactly once, repeats served from cache, and every failure traced and explained. It works for any agent workload. This page measures it on one example: a six-agent team that plans and books a trip.
